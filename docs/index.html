What is Fractional Ownership of Space?

A revolutionary way to invest in real estate — one square foot at a time

📐

Buy by the Square Foot

Purchase shares of one square foot or more in premium properties. No need to buy an entire unit — own exactly the space you want.

🏛️

Special Purpose Vehicle

Each property is held under a dedicated SPV structure, ensuring transparent ownership, legal clarity, and professional management.

🔑

Not UDS — It's FoS

Unlike traditional Undivided Share of Land, FoS gives you fractional ownership of actual built-up space in the property.

💰

Affordable Entry

Start with a minimal advance and own a share in Chennai's prime real estate. Real estate investing, made accessible.

How It Works

Simple steps to own your fractional space

1

Choose a Property

Browse our curated list of premium properties across Chennai and select the one that fits your investment goals.

2

Select Your Space

Decide how many square feet of space you want to own — starting from just one square foot.

3

Pay Your Advance

Secure your fractional ownership by paying the advance. Your share is locked in under the SPV structure.

4

Own & Earn

Receive your ownership certificate and start earning returns from your fractional space in the property.
